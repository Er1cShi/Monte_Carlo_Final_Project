        We show that multigrid ideas can be used to reduce the computational complexity of estimating an expected value arising
        from a stochastic differential equation using Monte Carlo path simulations. In the simplest case of a Lipschitz payoff and
        a Euler discretisation, the computational cost to achieve an accuracy of O is reduced from O−3  to O−2 log 2 .
        The analysis is supported by numerical results showing signiﬁcant computational savings.
        Subject classiﬁcations: analysis of algorithms: computational complexity; ﬁnance; simulation: efﬁciency.
        Area of review: Financial Engineering.
